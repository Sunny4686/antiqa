# Revenue/conversation intelligence platforms competing with or resembling Agaton (status October 2026)

Method note: about 15 web searches and fetch attempts on 2026-10-17. Direct fetches of help.gong.io and tldv.io failed (DNS blocked), so most findings come from search snippets. Many pricing sources are competitor or affiliate blogs (Sybill, Claap, Avoma, Revenue.io, MarketBetter, CostBench, Outdoo, Demodesk) and are marked as such. I found no vendor-published list prices for Gong, Chorus, Outreach/Kaia, Clari Copilot, Attention, Jiminny, Hyperbound or Second Nature. Anything not backed by a source is in the Gaps sections.

## Q0. Vendor profiles (reference point: Agaton) and feature matrix

### Takeaway
Agaton is a post-call/near-real-time analyzer of large volumes of phone calls (sales and customer service) for Nordic enterprises (telco, lending). Its most direct global analogues are Gong, Chorus and Jiminny. Most US tools (Avoma, Sybill, Attention, Fireflies, Otter) are meeting/video-first notetakers, not phone-contact-center analytics. The market has merged into "Revenue Action Orchestration" (Gartner), and in 2025–2026 the push is toward agents (CRM autofill, custom agents, MCP).

## Q1. Which features are table stakes vs differentiators in 2026?

### Takeaway
Recording, transcription, summary, search, CRM sync and basic coaching are table stakes even at $10–20/seat (Fireflies/Otter/Avoma). Differentiation has moved to agents that act (CRM autofill, custom agents, MCP), forecasting/orchestration, real-time assistance, and coaching/roleplay before the call.

### Cited Findings
- Gartner created a new category, Revenue Action Orchestration (first MQ Dec 2025), as sales engagement and revenue intelligence converge. CI is evaluated as part of it, not separately — [Gong press](https://www.gong.io/press/gong-named-a-leader-in-2025-gartner-magic-quadrant-for-revenue-action-orchestration); [BusinessWire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20251216171118/en/Clari-Recognized-as-a-Leader-and-Salesloft-a-Visionary-in-the-First-2025-Gartner-Magic-Quadrant-for-Revenue-Action-Orchestration)
- Leaders: Clari, Gong, Outreach — [Spotlight AR (third party)](https://research.oz.spotlightar.com/reports/magic-quadrant-revenue-action-orchestration-2025/leaders)
- Agent race: Gong Custom Agents/Agent Builder/MCP — [Gong press](https://gong.io/press/gong-named-to-fast-company-s-annual-list-of-the-world-s-most-innovative-companies-for-2026); Salesloft MCP server (Apr 2026) — [Claap](https://www.claap.io/blog/clari-competitors); Sybill MCP/CRM autofill — [Claap](https://www.claap.io/blog/sybill-alternatives); Attention raising $30M to go from "recording to running" — [Attention](https://www.attention.com/blog-posts/attention-series-b-press-release); [Future Nexus](https://www.heyfuturenexus.com/posts/funded-attention-raises-30m-as-ai-sales-software-shifts-from-note-taking-to-action)

- Notetaker pricing pressure: Fireflies Business $19, Otter Business $19.99 — [CostBench](https://costbench.com/compare/otter-ai-vs-fireflies-ai/)

### Inferences
- The table-stakes/differentiator split is my synthesis of the price and feature levels above, not an analyst source.
- For a newcomer like Agaton, local language quality, phone volume and vertical outcome data (conversion, QA time) are more credible differentiators than general "agents", where Gong and Salesloft set the pace.

## Q2. Which support Swedish well? Complaints about Nordic language quality?

### Takeaway
Gong has the clearest documented Swedish support (its own "hyper-optimized" model, with some features excluded). Modjo and Jiminny list Swedish. Sybill translates to English, Avoma appears to lack Swedish, and Otter probably lacks it. No concrete G2/Reddit complaints about Swedish quality were found, but there are general signals that Swedish transcription is "patchy" with some providers.

### Cited Findings
- Gong: Swedish and Danish "hyper-optimized". Feature exceptions for Swedish: next steps, consent page, audio prompt, pre-call email — [Gong Help](https://help.gong.io/docs/what-languages-are-supported-in-gong)
- Modjo: Swedish and Danish on Capterra's language list; "#1 for European languages" (own claim); a competitor mentions accuracy problems with some accents — [Capterra](https://www.capterra.in/software/215663/modjo); [Demodesk (competitor)](https://demodesk.com/comparison/demodesk-vs-modjo)
- Jiminny: 60+ languages via external partners; the English model is its own showcase — [Jiminny Help](https://help.jiminny.com/en_US/transcription/how-jiminny-transcription-works)
- Sybill: Swedish supported but not on the "top accuracy" list; output is translated to English — [Sybill Help](https://help.sybill.ai/en/articles/10473150-supported-languages-in-sybill)
- Avoma: English; Swedish requested by customers — [Avoma Help](https://help.avoma.com/does-avoma-support-other-languages)
- Otter: reportedly only EN/FR/ES (or a few more), not Swedish — [Circleback](https://circleback.ai/compare/fireflies-ai-vs-otter-ai)
- tl;dv's (competitor) Swedish test: tl;dv 188/200, Swedish Klang 166, Fathom and HappyScribe lower. "Feedback from Swedish speakers indicates that the accuracy … have been patchy with particular providers" — [tl;dv](https://tldv.io/blog/swedish-meeting-transcription-accuracy/) (search snippet only; fetch failed)
- Dynamics: Swedish confirmed for Copilot in Sales, not for CI — [Microsoft Learn](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-gb/dynamics365/sales/faq-conversation-intelligence)

### Inferences
- Even where Swedish is supported, features are often missing (Gong) or analysis runs via English translation (Sybill). That leaves room for a Swedish-first player like Agaton, especially on phone audio (8 kHz), dialects and Swedish-English code-switching. These are expected weak points, but no source confirms them.

### Gaps
- No independent WER measurements for Swedish/Norwegian/Danish/Finnish for any of the vendors.
- No Reddit/G2 reviews found that specifically complain about Nordic languages (searches gave no hits).

## Q3. Pricing levels and typical contract structures

### Takeaway
Three tiers: enterprise RI (Gong, Chorus, Salesloft/Clari, Outreach) at about $100–250/seat/month plus a platform fee, annual or multi-year contracts and bundling. Mid-market (Sybill, Avoma, Modjo, Jiminny, Clari Copilot) at about $30–110. Notetakers (Fireflies/Otter) at $8–39 with public list prices.

### Cited Findings
- Gong: per seat plus a platform fee of $5k–50k/year (raised to up to $50k in March 2025 per one source), about $7.5k implementation. Seats can't be reduced during the term, 5–8% (or 5–15%) annual uplifts, early exit penalties of 50–100% of remaining value. New contracts quote about $250/user/month for mandatory bundles vs historical about $160 — [MarketBetter](https://marketbetter.ai/blog/gong-pricing-breakdown-2026/); [Sybill (competitor)](https://sybill.ai/blogs/gong-pricing); [RaftLabs](https://www.raftlabs.com/blog/gong-pricing)
- Chorus bundled with ZoomInfo at $35–80k/year for 10 users — [MarketBetter](https://marketbetter.ai/blog/chorus-ai-pricing-breakdown-2026)
- Clari Copilot $720–1,320/rep/year — [TrustRadius](https://tr-web.dev.trustradius.com/compare-products/wingman)
- Sybill $30/$90, Avoma $19 + $29 add-ons, Fireflies $10–39, Otter $8.33–19.99 — see Q0 sources
- Modjo bills only active licenses (own claim); possibly a 15-seat minimum (competitor claim) — [Modjo](https://en.modjo.ai/alternatives/gong); [Demodesk](https://demodesk.com/comparison/demodesk-vs-modjo)
- HubSpot Sales Pro $90–100/seat plus a $1,500 onboarding fee — [HubSpot](https://www.hubspot.com/pricing/sales)
- Second Nature est. $30–40/seat, about a $20k annual minimum (unconfirmed) — [Outdoo](https://www.outdoo.ai/blog/second-nature-review-and-pricing)

### Inferences
- In this category, enterprise contracts are typically annual and prepaid with seat lock-in. That makes Nordic buyers (e.g. telcos with many customer-service agents) sensitive to per-seat models. Volume/minute-based pricing could be a lever for Agaton (Agaton's pricing model unknown).

### Gaps
- No list prices found for Jiminny, Attention, Hyperbound, Kaia or Dynamics CI. Many figures come from competitor blogs and conflict with each other.

## Q4. Where are customers dissatisfied (price, complexity, accuracy, privacy)?

### Takeaway
Price and contract rigidity dominate (especially Gong). After that: bundling and complexity, adoption problems in small teams, data export, and weaker accuracy for accents and non-English languages. Privacy is mostly about consent/disclosure and how managers use clips, not about technical security.

### Cited Findings
- Gong: price, forced bundling, seat lock, weak API export. Enterprise teams (250+ reps) see ROI, small and mid-sized teams struggle with adoption and justifying cost — [Sybill (competitor) G2 analysis](https://www.sybill.ai/blogs/gong-reviews); [MarketBetter review](https://marketbetter.ai/blog/gong-review-2026/)
- A G2 reviewer calls Gong Engage weak (missing task APIs, poor integration with other dialers) — [Sybill (competitor)](https://sybill.ai/blogs/gong-pricing)
- Salesloft/Clari: integration not finished, separate interfaces, no unified roadmap, pricing moving upward (competitor claims) — [Claap](https://www.claap.io/blog/clari-competitors); [Revenue.io](https://www.revenue.io/blog/the-salesloft-and-clari-merger-what-it-means-for-your-sales-team)
- Fireflies: AI credits run out, leading to add-on costs — [Sonix](https://sonix.ai/resources/de/fireflies-ai-pricing/). Otter: "absolutely horrible at capturing multi-speaker transcripts" (Reddit, quoted) — [aiproductivity.ai](https://aiproductivity.ai/blog/ai-transcription-accuracy/)
- Privacy: recording requires disclosure, and laws vary. Gong has SOC 2 Type 2/ISO, but "a manager who uses clips to embarrass people" is a problem certification doesn't solve. Reviewers note missed recordings despite consent — [ConsentStack](https://consentstack.io/vendors/gong); [Extruct](https://www.extruct.ai/hub/gong-io/); [AWS Marketplace reviews](https://aws.amazon.com/marketplace/reviews/reviews-list/prodview-lgmuw6vybtvpo?page=128)
- European buyers value GDPR/EU hosting as built-in (Modjo) — [Puzzle Inbox](https://puzzleinbox.com/tools/modjo)

### Inferences
- For the Nordic market, GDPR, EU data residency and Swedish quality are likely purchasing criteria where US vendors are weaker or less documented. This is an inference, not verified against Nordic buyers.

### Gaps
- No direct r/sales threads found (search returned only vendor pages). Primary G2/Capterra review text could not be read; complaints are mostly reported via competitor blogs.
